About Us

Capturing love. Preserving legacy.

For over 10 years, Thilanka Dilshan Photography has been more than a photography brand. It has been a trusted storyteller of love, emotion, and unforgettable beginnings.

The Story

A decade of trust, passion and excellence.

Every glance, every smile, and every tear of joy is carefully preserved as a timeless memory that will be cherished for generations.

We believe that your wedding is not just an event. It is the beginning of a legacy. Our passion lies in transforming fleeting moments into artistic masterpieces that tell your story with authenticity, elegance, and heart.
